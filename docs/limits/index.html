What happens at the limit

A rate-limited request gets 429 with a Retry-After header in seconds. The SDKs honour it. We do not drop events for being over the rate limit — we ask you to slow down and keep the ones you already sent.

Raising a limit

Rate limits can be raised on Scale and Enterprise; ask support. Payload and batch sizes are fixed for everyone.
