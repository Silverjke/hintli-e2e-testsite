Webhooks

Events we send

  • threshold.reached — a customer crossed an alert threshold.
  • period.closed — a billing period is final and safe to invoice.
  • correction.created — a closed period changed and needs approval.
  • customer.silent — an active customer stopped sending events.

Retries

Any response other than 2xx is retried five times over 24 hours: after 1 minute, 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours and 12 hours. After that the delivery is marked failed and kept for seven days so you can replay it.

Signatures

Every request carries X-Acme-Signature, an HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body with your endpoint secret, and X-Acme-Timestamp. Reject anything older than five minutes and compare signatures in constant time.

Ordering

Not guaranteed. Each payload carries a sequence number per customer; ignore a lower one than you have already processed.
