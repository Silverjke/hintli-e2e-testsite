Product overview

Acme Telemetry is four stages in one pipeline. You can adopt them in order; most teams start with ingest and transform, then turn on monitoring once the numbers match their old reports.

The four stages

  1. Ingest — get events in, without losing any.
  2. Transform — turn events into billable quantities.
  3. Monitor — see usage per customer as it happens.
  4. Alerts — act before the invoice does.

What it is not

We do not issue invoices, take payments, or handle tax. We hand clean quantities to the billing system you already run — Stripe, Chargebee, Recurly, or your own. That boundary is deliberate: billing systems are hard to replace and we are not asking you to.

Where the data lives

You choose EU (Frankfurt) or US (Virginia) when you create the workspace. The region cannot be changed afterwards without a migration, which support runs for you at no charge.
