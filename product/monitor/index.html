Monitor

One screen per customer, updated within thirty seconds of an event arriving.

What you see

  • Usage against plan entitlement, for the current period.
  • Projected end-of-period usage, from the trailing seven days.
  • The events behind any number — click a quantity, get the rows.

Reconciliation

A daily job compares what we metered against what your billing system charged and flags any line that differs by more than 0.5%. Most teams find their first real discrepancy in week one; it is usually a meter that counted retries.

Retention

Raw events are kept 7 days on Starter, 30 days on Growth and 90 days on Scale. Aggregated quantities are kept for 24 months on every plan, including after you cancel.
