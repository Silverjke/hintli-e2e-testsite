Ingest

Three ways in, all of them idempotent.

HTTP

POST a JSON event to /v1/events. Single events or batches of up to 1,000. Every event carries an idempotency_key; we keep keys for 48 hours and silently drop duplicates inside that window.

SDKs

Official libraries for Node, Python, Go and Ruby. Each buffers in memory and flushes every two seconds or every 500 events, whichever comes first, and retries with exponential backoff.

Warehouse sync

If usage already lands in Postgres, Snowflake or BigQuery, point us at a table or a view and we poll it on a schedule you set, minimum every five minutes.

Guarantees

  • At-least-once delivery, with idempotency keys to make it effectively exactly-once.
  • Events accepted up to 72 hours late and still attributed to the period they belong to.
  • Maximum payload 256 KB per request.

Rate limits are on the limits page.
