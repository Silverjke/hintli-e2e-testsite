Transform

A meter is a named rule that turns events into one billable quantity per customer per period.

Meter types

  • Count — how many events matched.
  • Sum — add a numeric field, for example bytes processed.
  • Max — the peak in the period, for seats or concurrent workers.
  • Unique — distinct values of a field, for monthly active users.

Why rules, not code

Every meter is a short declaration your finance team can read without asking an engineer what it does. When a customer disputes a line, you can show them the rule.

Backfills and corrections

Change a meter and we recompute the open period immediately. Closed periods are never changed silently: you get a correction record you can approve or discard, and approved corrections appear as a separate line so nobody's invoice history rewrites itself.
