Alerts

Thresholds on any meter, delivered where your team already looks.

Triggers

  • Percentage of entitlement — commonly 80% and 100%.
  • Absolute quantity — for example 5 million events.
  • Rate of change — usage more than doubling week over week.
  • Silence — a customer that normally sends events stops for six hours.

Destinations

Email, Slack, webhook, or PagerDuty. Webhooks retry five times over 24 hours; the schedule is on the webhooks page.

The silence alert

The one people underuse. A customer who stops sending events has usually broken their integration, and they will find out at renewal unless somebody tells them. It has saved more accounts for our customers than the overage alerts have.
