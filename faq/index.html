Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to set up?

Most teams send a first event in under twenty minutes and have their first meter matching their old numbers within a week. The slow part is agreeing what a billable unit is, not the code.

Do I have to replace my billing system?

No. We produce quantities; your billing system charges for them. We work with Stripe, Chargebee, Recurly, and anything that takes an API call.

What happens if you go down?

SDKs buffer and retry, and we accept events up to 72 hours late attributed to the right period, so a short outage costs you nothing but patience. Uptime commitments are in the SLA.

Can I try it against real traffic?

Yes. Every workspace has a sandbox that takes the same events and never bills. Teams usually run sandbox and production side by side for a month.

Do you support usage-based and seat-based billing together?

Yes. A max meter over an active-seat event gives you seats; count and sum meters give you usage.

Who owns the data?

You do. Export any time as CSV or Parquet; on cancellation we keep aggregates for 24 months and delete raw events within 30 days.

Is there an on-premise version?

No, and there is no plan for one. Enterprise customers can choose the region and get a single tenant database, which covers most of what people actually mean when they ask.

How do I reach a human?

Email works on every plan. Growth gets four-hour responses in business hours, Scale gets a shared Slack channel. See contact.
