Usage metering your finance team will actually trust

Acme Telemetry collects usage events from your product, aggregates them into billable quantities, and hands them to your billing system. One pipeline, one source of truth, no month-end spreadsheet archaeology.

What it does

  • Ingest — send events over HTTP, from our SDKs, or straight out of your warehouse.
  • Transform — turn raw events into billable quantities with rules you can read.
  • Monitor — watch usage per customer in real time, before the invoice surprises anyone.
  • Alert — tell a customer they are at 80% of their plan while it still matters.

Who it is for

Teams that bill on consumption: API companies, infrastructure, data platforms, and anyone who has outgrown a nightly SQL job. Most customers are between $1M and $50M in annual revenue and have two to twenty engineers touching billing.

Getting started

A 14-day trial needs no credit card and includes everything on the Growth plan. Most teams send their first event within twenty minutes of signing up — see the quickstart. If you would rather see it first, book a walkthrough.
