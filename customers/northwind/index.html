Northwind Data

Northwind Data is a fictional company used in this case study.

Before

Three engineers spent the first week of every month reconciling a nightly aggregation job against Stripe. Disputes took days because nobody could show the events behind a line.

What they did

They ran our sandbox beside the old job for six weeks, compared daily, and found two real defects in the old job: retried API calls were counted twice, and a timezone bug moved roughly 0.4% of usage into the wrong month.

After

  • Month-end close went from five working days to one.
  • Billing disputes fell by about two thirds, because support can show the events.
  • They found $40,000 a year of usage they had not been billing at all.

They are on the Scale plan with a warehouse sync from Snowflake.
