Privacy policy

Last updated 1 September.

Who we are

Acme Telemetry is the data controller for account data and the data processor for the event data you send us.

Account data

Name, work email, company, and billing details. Kept while the account is open and for six years afterwards, because tax law requires it for invoices.

Event data

Whatever you send. We recommend you send identifiers, not personal data; the fields are yours to choose. Retention follows your plan: 7, 30 or 90 days for raw events, 24 months for aggregates.

Website

First-party analytics only, no third-party advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking. We keep web logs for 30 days.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, portability and objection, as set out in the GDPR. Email the privacy address on the contact page; we answer within 30 days.

Transfers

EU workspaces stay in the EU. US workspaces stay in the US. Where a subprocessor requires a transfer, we rely on standard contractual clauses.

Acme Telemetry is a fictional product. This site exists only to test an AI assistant end to end. No real company, people, customers or prices are described, and nothing here can be bought.
