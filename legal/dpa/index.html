Data processing addendum

This addendum forms part of the terms of service and needs no separate signature, though we will sign a copy on request.

Roles

You are the controller of the event data; we are the processor. Where we determine the purpose — account administration, billing — we are the controller.

Instructions

We process event data only on your documented instructions, which are the configuration in your workspace and any written request from an account admin.

Confidentiality

Everyone with access is under a written confidentiality obligation that survives their employment.

Security measures

As set out on the security page, which forms part of this addendum.

Subprocessors

Listed here, with 30 days' notice of additions and a right to object.

Breach notification

We notify you without undue delay and in any case within 48 hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting your data, with what we know at the time.

Audits

Our SOC 2 Type II report satisfies audit rights. Enterprise customers may run one on-site audit per year with 30 days' notice, at their own cost.

Deletion

On termination, raw events within 30 days, aggregates within 24 months, or sooner on request.
