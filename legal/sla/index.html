Service level agreement

Applies to Growth, Scale and Enterprise. Starter has no uptime commitment.

The commitment

99.9% uptime per calendar month, measured on the events endpoint and the dashboard, excluding scheduled maintenance.

Credits

Monthly uptimeCredit
Below 99.9% and at or above 99.0%10% of that month's fee
Below 99.0% and at or above 95.0%25% of that month's fee
Below 95.0%50% of that month's fee

Credits apply to the next invoice. They are the only remedy for missed uptime. Ask within 30 days of the end of the affected month; we do not apply them automatically.

Scheduled maintenance

At most four hours per month, announced at least 72 hours ahead, in a window between 01:00 and 05:00 UTC on a Sunday. Ingest keeps accepting events during maintenance.

What is excluded

Problems caused by your own code or network, force majeure, and any suspension for non-payment or acceptable-use breach.
