Security

Certifications

SOC 2 Type II, audited annually; the current report covers the twelve months to 31 March. Customers under NDA can request it from their account contact. ISO 27001 is in progress with certification expected next year. We are not HIPAA eligible and do not accept PHI.

Data residency

You pick EU (Frankfurt) or US (Virginia) when the workspace is created. Data does not leave the chosen region, including backups. Support staff access is region-restricted.

Encryption

TLS 1.3 in transit, with TLS 1.2 accepted for older clients. AES-256 at rest, keys managed by the cloud provider's KMS and rotated yearly.

Access control

Roles are owner, admin, analyst and read-only. SSO with SAML 2.0 and SCIM provisioning are available on Scale and Enterprise. Audit logs cover every configuration change and every export, and are retained for one year.

Retention and deletion

Raw events follow your plan's retention — 7, 30 or 90 days. Aggregates are kept 24 months. On cancellation raw events are deleted within 30 days and aggregates within 24 months, or immediately on written request.

Vulnerability handling

Report to the security address on the contact page. We acknowledge within one business day and aim to fix critical issues within seven days. We run an external penetration test annually and share the summary letter on request.

Subprocessors

The current list is on the subprocessors page. We give 30 days' notice before adding one.
