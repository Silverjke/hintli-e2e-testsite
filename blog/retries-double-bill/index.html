Retries are quietly double-billing you

The single most common defect we see in a home-grown pipeline: a client retries, the server logs both attempts, and the customer pays twice.

Why it hides

Retries are a small share of traffic, so the error is a percent or two — under the noise floor of a monthly total, and invisible until a customer with a flaky network complains.

How to find it

Group last month by customer and by minute, and look for identical payloads inside a few seconds. If the count is more than a rounding error, you have been billing for it.

The fix

An idempotency key generated by the client, not the server, and kept long enough to cover the retry window. Ours live 48 hours.
