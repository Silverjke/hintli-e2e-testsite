Billing FAQ

What counts as one event?

One accepted JSON object in the events endpoint. A rejected event does not count. A duplicate caught by its idempotency key does not count. A batch of 500 counts as 500.

What happens when I upgrade mid-month?

The new plan starts immediately and we charge the difference, pro-rated to the day. Your included events reset to the new plan's allowance for the remainder of the period.

And when I downgrade?

Downgrades take effect at the start of your next period. We never pro-rate a refund for a downgrade.

Can I pay annually but add seats monthly?

Seats are included in the plan, so on Starter and Growth you would move up a plan. Scale has unlimited seats.

Do you take purchase orders?

On annual plans of $2,400 or more, yes. Net 30 by default; Net 60 with a signed agreement.

Who handles tax?

Invoices are issued from our Irish entity. EU customers with a valid VAT number are reverse charged; everyone else pays local VAT. US customers are charged sales tax where we have nexus.

What currency?

US dollars and euros. Euro prices are €45, €229 and €829 per month for Starter, Growth and Scale. The currency is fixed when the workspace is created.

Is there a discount for startups or non-profits?

Registered non-profits get 50% off any plan. Companies under two years old with less than $1M raised get Growth at Starter's price for twelve months.

What if I dispute an invoice?

Email billing within 30 days. We freeze the disputed line, not your account, while we check it.
